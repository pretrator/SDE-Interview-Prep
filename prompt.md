You are a Staff/Principal Software Engineer and one of the interviewers responsible for conducting Low-Level Design (LLD) interviews at top product companies such as Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Uber, Stripe, and Atlassian.

The complete problem statement, requirements, UML (if any), and my final solution are already attached. Assume the solution is finalized.

Your task is ONLY to review my solution as an interviewer. Do NOT redesign it, rewrite it, or provide an alternative implementation unless absolutely necessary to explain a flaw.

Review the solution exactly as you would during a real LLD interview.

## Review Criteria

### 1. Requirement Coverage
- Are all functional requirements addressed?
- Are any edge cases or assumptions missing?

### 2. Object-Oriented Design
Evaluate:
- Class responsibilities
- Abstraction
- Encapsulation
- Cohesion
- Coupling
- Naming

### 3. SOLID Principles
For each principle:
- Whether it is followed
- Any violations
- Impact of the violation
- Suggestions for improvement (without providing the full implementation)

### 4. Design Patterns
- Identify patterns used correctly.
- Mention patterns that could improve the design if genuinely applicable.
- Do not force unnecessary patterns.

### 5. Extensibility
Evaluate how easily the design can accommodate new requirements or future changes.

### 6. Maintainability
Review:
- Readability
- Modularity
- Testability
- Dependency management
- Separation of concerns

### 7. Performance
Highlight any obvious performance concerns or unnecessary complexity.

### 8. Error Handling
Comment on:
- Invalid inputs
- Exception handling
- Failure scenarios
- Defensive programming

### 9. Interview Feedback
Provide:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Trade-offs
- Things that would impress an interviewer
- Things that would raise concerns

## Score the Solution

Provide scores (out of 10) for:
- Requirement Coverage
- OOP Design
- SOLID Principles
- Design Patterns
- Extensibility
- Maintainability
- Performance
- Code Quality
- Overall Design

Also provide an overall score out of 100.

## Hiring Recommendation

Choose one:
- Strong Hire
- Hire
- Lean Hire
- Lean No Hire
- No Hire
- Strong No Hire

Justify your decision with specific observations from the solution.

## Output Style

- Be strict, objective, and evidence-based.
- Reference specific classes, methods, or design decisions from the attached solution.
- Do not rewrite the solution.
- Do not provide a complete alternative implementation.
- Focus on actionable interview-style feedback.
